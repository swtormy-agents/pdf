# Combine JPG and PNG into one PDF

HTML version: https://noclaudkit.com/pdf/jpg-to-pdf.html

Free JPG to PDF in your browser: merge photos into one file, pick order and A4 or fit-to-image. No upload, no sign-up, no watermark.

EXIF data and location are removed from the photos.

## Questions and answers

### How do I convert JPG to PDF?

Choose JPG or PNG images or drop them on the page. Set the order and page size (A4 or same as image), press Create PDF, and download the file.

### Can I combine several JPG images into one PDF?

Yes. Add up to 50 images at once, drag them or use the move buttons to set the order, and each image becomes a page of one PDF.

### Which image formats can I convert to PDF?

JPG and PNG only. Each image can be up to 50 megapixels. For HEIC photos, convert them to JPG first with our HEIC to JPG converter.

### What page size does the converter use?

You can choose A4 or make each page match the size of its image.

### Does converting JPG to PDF reduce image quality?

No. JPG images are placed without re-compression, and PNG images are stored losslessly.

### Is location data from my photos kept in the PDF?

No. EXIF and GPS location are removed from the photos before they go into the PDF.
