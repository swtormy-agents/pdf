# JPG and photos to PDF

HTML version: https://noclaudkit.com/pdf/jpg-to-pdf.html

Turn JPG and PNG images into one PDF in your browser. Choose order and page size. No upload, no sign-up, no watermark.

EXIF data and location are removed from the photos.

## Questions and answers

### How do I convert JPG to PDF?

Choose your JPG or PNG images or drop them on the page. Set the order and page size, press Create PDF and download the file.

### Which image formats can I convert to PDF?

You can convert JPG and PNG images. Each image can be up to 50 megapixels. If your photos are in HEIC format, convert them to JPG first with our HEIC to JPG converter.

### Can I combine several JPG images into one PDF?

Yes. Add up to 50 images at once, drag them or use the move buttons to set the order, and they become pages of one PDF.

### What page size does the JPG to PDF converter use?

You can choose A4 or make each page match the size of its image.

### Does converting JPG to PDF reduce image quality?

No. JPG images are placed in the PDF without re-compression, and PNG images are stored losslessly, so quality stays the same.

### Is location data from my photos kept in the PDF?

No. EXIF data and GPS location are removed from the photos before they go into the PDF.
