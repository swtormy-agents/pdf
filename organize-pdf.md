# Reorder, delete and rotate PDF pages

HTML version: https://noclaudkit.com/pdf/organize-pdf.html

Drag pages to rearrange a PDF, delete or rotate pages, then save. Runs in your browser, files are never uploaded. Free, no watermark.

## Questions and answers

### How do I reorder PDF pages?

Add your PDF and drag the pages into the order you want, or use the move buttons on each page. Then save the PDF with the new page order.

### Can I rearrange PDF pages with the keyboard?

Yes. Select a page, press Space to pick it up, move it with the arrow keys and press Space again to drop it. Press Esc to cancel a move.

### How do I delete pages from a PDF?

Press the delete button on a page, or select it and press Delete. You can undo it for 10 seconds after you delete.

### How do I rotate PDF pages?

Press the rotate button on a page, or select it and press R. Each press turns the page 90 degrees clockwise, and the rotation is saved in the file.

### Will reordering PDF pages change their quality?

No. Page text and images are moved into the new file without re-encoding. Form fields (including anything typed into them), attachments, JavaScript and document metadata are not carried over, so keep the original if you need the form.
